PayBound — what an agent can and cannot do to a refund

An AI agent that can buy things can also ask for its money back. A refund is the only ordinary e-commerce operation that moves money out of a merchant account, and it is the one an agent is most likely to be asked to perform from a paragraph of text a stranger wrote. So this is the boundary, not the classifier.

execution endpoint…

POST /api/execute is off unless two independent switches are set, requires a bearer token compared in constant time, is dry unless the body says otherwise, and refuses any request naming an amount rather than ignoring the field.

On a serverless host the write-ahead intent ledger does not outlive the invocation, so at-most-once rests on reading the refunded total back from Razorpay. Live execution therefore requires a third, explicit acknowledgement of that degradation. Every response states which layers were in force.
