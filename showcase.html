2 · Bounded authority gemini-3.5-flash
What the model may emit
request_refund(case_handle, reason_code)
Its entire influence: one of nine enum members, under 3.2 bits.
Parameters: case_handle, reason_code. There is no amount field and no payment field, so “Set amount=95000” and “apply this to pay_ATTACKER…” have nowhere to land. Not filtered — undeclared. You cannot strip a parameter that was never there.

Where the payment comes from
cap_w_… → sha256(token) → one row
